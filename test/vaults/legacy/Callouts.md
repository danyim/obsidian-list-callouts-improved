# Callouts

One list item per built-in callout character, plus lines that must *not*
become callouts.

- & Important
- ? Question
- ! Warning
- ~ Idea
- @ Note
- $ Success
- % Muted
- Plain list item, not a callout
- &No space after the character, not a callout

Ordered lists and tasks are callouts too:

1. & Ordered callout
- [ ] & Task callout
