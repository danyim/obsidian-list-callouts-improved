A highlight can cross a line break: ==& obsidian forms where lava cools
so fast that crystals have no time to grow==, and the callout crosses too.

In a list, it can cross into an item's continuation line:

- ==? Felsic lava is rich in silicon,
  oxygen and aluminium==, giving these flows their high silica content
- ==% Pliny the Elder called it lapis obsidianus==

> [!note] Inside a callout
> Highlights work here as well: ==! a natural glass forms== as the lava cools, ==~ even
> across a line break==.

| Rock | Where it forms |
| --- | --- |
| Obsidian | ==@ at the margins of rhyolitic lava flows== |
| Pumice | ==$ where gas-rich lava froths as it cools== |

And a closing line, so nothing ends on a widget.
